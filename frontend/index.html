What the audit means

Exact feature matches with conflicting labels do not prove annotation mistakes: selected features may omit distinguishing context. Duplicate-aware development splits reduce one form of leakage; they do not provide reliable session or temporal separation.

Flow explanation

Positive contributions push the model toward attack; negative contributions push toward normal. Contributions add in log-odds, not probability. This explains model behavior, not causality or a confirmed incident.
